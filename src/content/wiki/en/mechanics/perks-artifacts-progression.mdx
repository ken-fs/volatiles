Volatiles' progression story is told in three places: a perks panel, an artifacts panel, and a tank full of water. This page collects what the announcement material actually shows about how a run grows and what death gives back — everything checked against the official screenshots and store description as of **October 11, 2026**.

## What are perks?

The tile-selection screenshot shows a **PERKS** column on the right side of the screen, and separately, a **"LEVEL UP"** marker floating in the arena during combat. Read together, the in-run growth loop is the genre standard: fight, level up, choose a perk, repeat. The perk system is one of the two reward axes you build a run's power curve on.

What is not visible yet: the perk pool size, whether upgrades are points-based or card-choice-based, and whether perks are tied to specific weapons. Expect the classic roguelike three-choice-screen once gameplay footage arrives; do not treat that as confirmed.

## What are artifacts?

Beside PERKS, the same screenshot shows an **ARTIFACTS** column, currently locked (door-like icons). In the roguelike vocabulary, two readings dominate:

1. **In-run pickups** — powerful items you assemble during a run, the archetype being Binding of Isaac's item synergies. The locked icons would then be slots you fill as you find artifacts.
2. **Meta unlocks** — permanent equipment kept between runs, unlocked via the lab. The locked/framed presentation and the "lab is studying you" framing support this reading too.

Most likely it is in-run items *seeded by* meta unlocks — the layered pattern (Hades' boons, Dead Cells' blueprints) — but that synthesis is inference, clearly labelled as such.

## How does the death loop work?

This part is confirmed, because the store description says it outright: *"when you fail… wake up in the cold, water-filled tank with new knowledge that might save your life next time."*

So the meta loop is:

- **You die in the nightmare** → cut to the lab tank.
- **You wake with "new knowledge"** — the framing for meta progression. In genre terms: anything unlocked or improved here persists.
- **You go deeper next time** — "each run sends you deeper into a nightmare experiment", implying progression is also measured as depth reached, not just power gained.

The narrative engine under all of this: *"strangers in a covert lab are watching closely. And whatever is growing in your mind is learning how to take control."* At minimum that is flavor text; at most it hints the game adapts to your habits — an "enemy learning" system is a known roguelike device, and the wording is suspiciously specific. Treat the adaptation theory as a theory until the game ships. See [everything we know](/guides/everything-we-know/) for the sourced facts.

## What grows during a run versus between runs?

Assembling the visible pieces into a working model (labelled as model, not fact):

| Layer | Grows during a run | Persists after death |
|---|---|---|
| Weapons | Yes — slots show Lv0 → Lv2 within screenshots | Unclear |
| Character | "LEVEL UP" appears in-run | Unclear |
| Perks | Panel exists in-run | Likely partially, via "knowledge" |
| Artifacts | Panel exists, locked slots | Likely partially, via "knowledge" |
| Arena/tiles | Tile choices shape each run's land | New tile types likely unlocked |
| Objective progress | Capsule count runs (7/9, 8/9) | Possibly — see [combat breakdown](/mechanics/combat-and-waves/) |

The double question marks are the honest state of things. The announcement revealed a concept, not a systems spreadsheet.
