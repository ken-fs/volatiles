There is no Volatiles release date yet. As of **October 31, 2026**, the Steam page still says "Coming soon" (we checked it today), with no year given.

The game was announced on October 8 with a trailer and a wishlist button, nothing more. 11 bit studios usually gives dates much later. Below is how to find out the moment it happens.

## What did the announcement say about timing?

Exactly one thing: nothing. The press release, Steam page, and all day-one coverage (Gematsu, CGMagazine) confirm only "in development" with a wishlist prompt. If you see a release date claimed anywhere right now, it is invented — including any specific year.

## How does 11 bit studios usually announce dates?

Their pattern with published and internal titles (Frostpunk 2, The Alters, Moonlighter 2) is: silence, then a dated **announcement trailer or press release**, usually paired with a Steam page refresh and a preorder or demo offer. For a brand-new studio like Team Instant Defeat, expect the same pipeline — the publisher controls the marketing calendar, not the developer's social accounts.

## What signals should you watch?

1. **The Steam page** — the "Coming soon" box changes to a concrete date first. If you wishlist it, Steam itself emails you on reveal, sale, and release. This is the one notification that cannot lag behind.
2. **The 11 bit studios YouTube channel** — release-date trailers land there same-day; the announcement trailer is already on it.
3. **Press release wires** — GamesPress carried the announcement; dated follow-ups will appear there and get mirrored by Gematsu and friends within hours.
4. **Demo or playtest surprises** — 11 bit sometimes ships a demo before a date announcement (the industry's Next Fest pattern). A demo appearing is the strongest signal that the date is close.

## What can we guess (and why you should distrust guesses)?

The honest position: **a 2026 release is unlikely** — a studio's first announcement with no window, in October, usually means 2027 or later. Games announced this way rarely ship within six months. But treat that as base rates, not information: publishers occasionally announce date and release within a quarter. The difference matters for you because it decides whether "start following now" means bookmarking or planning a site visit routine.
