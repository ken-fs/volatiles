[Volatiles](/guides/what-is-volatiles/) was announced on **October 8, 2026** — three days before this page was last checked. Everything below is sourced from the announcement itself (Steam page, press release, trailer); anything unconfirmed is labelled as such. We update this page as new information lands.

## The facts at a glance

- **Game:** Volatiles
- **Developer:** Team Instant Defeat (a newly established studio)
- **Publisher:** 11 bit studios
- **Genre:** Top-down action roguelike (Steam tags: Action, Adventure, Strategy)
- **Platform:** PC via Steam
- **Players:** Single-player only
- **Controller:** Full controller support, including DualSense and DualShock
- **Release date:** Not announced (details on the [Volatiles release date page](/guides/release-date/), checked October 31)
- **Price:** Not announced

## What is the premise?

You fight alien hordes inside a nightmare in your own mind, building the battlefield as you go. Each death sends you back to a water-filled tank in a covert lab, where the researchers are watching — and something in your mind is learning. The official tagline: *"every tile is important, and escaping with your sanity isn't guaranteed."*

## What has the publisher said?

The announcement press release (October 8) describes it as *"a fast-paced, top-down action roguelike where you build your own battlefield to survive an invasion within your mind."* 11 bit studios announced it as a new title joining their publishing portfolio, alongside developers they are known for: this is a publishing deal, not an internal 11 bit production — Team Instant Defeat is the developer.

## How much coverage has it gotten?

Reasonable day-one pickup for an indie announcement: Gematsu covered it with a full overview, CGMagazine ran an announcement piece, and the trailer is live on the 11 bit studios YouTube channel (roughly 13,000 views in the first three days, checked October 11). No release date was included in any coverage — several outlets explicitly note "release date not announced".
